## Prompt set

Three-slot master: Generate a true 90-degree overhead photographic view of the empty three-compartment base, using the existing three-slot product image as the construction reference. Match the walnut-effect frame, brown suede-like lining and pillows, antique-brass latch and tiny rear hinges. All outer edges must be exactly horizontal or vertical, with no perspective, visible exterior walls, tilt or rotation. Exactly three equal compartments in a single row, one centered upright pillow in each, two straight thin dividers. The glass lid is open upright and appears only as a thin hinge rail. No watches, labels, text, measurements, props, floor or external cast shadow. Actual transparent background, complete base in frame, realistic grain and lining under soft overhead light.

Six-slot version: Use the overhead master for viewpoint, finish and lighting, and the six-slot product photograph for the elongated base construction. Generate a wide rectangular orthographic overhead tray with exactly six equal compartments and six aligned upright pillows in one horizontal row. Five thin parallel dividers, brass clasp centered at the bottom, small hinges at the top. No large lid panel, glass covering the bays, watches or other objects. Straight, symmetric geometry, tight wide framing and a transparent background.

Single-slot concept: Use the overhead master and the matching single-box concept as references. Generate one narrow portrait rectangular base from directly above, with one centered elongated brown pillow, no divider, a bottom-center brass clasp and small rear hinges. Match the same wood effect, lining, microtexture, camera and overhead light. No perspective, exterior walls, tilt, watches, other cushions, branding, measurements or props. Transparent background and a slim clear margin around the complete tray.
